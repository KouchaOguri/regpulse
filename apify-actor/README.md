# RegPulse — FDA Warning Letters, openFDA Recalls & 40-Rule Amazon Claim Scanner (MCP-Ready)

Automated B2B regulatory intelligence Actor designed for **Amazon/Shopify operators, AI compliance agents (via Apify MCP Server), and brand aggregators**.

## Capabilities
1. **Full 40-Rule Listing Compliance Pre-Flight**: Screen up to 200 product SKUs against all 40 deterministic rules covering FDA drug & disease claims (21 CFR 101.93), OTC monographs (SPF, lidocaine, eye drops, anti-dandruff, oral care), prohibited ingredients (hydroquinone, CBD, BPC-157, SARMs, sildenafil, exosomes), pet CVM rules (21 CFR 510), medical device claims (21 CFR 807/820), and EPA FIFRA pesticide bot triggers — with automatic DSHEA disclaimer mitigation and safe rewrite guidance.
2. **Live FDA.gov Warning Letters Feed**: Filter 12-hour synced official FDA Warning Letters by vertical, OEM, brand, or ingredient (`https://regpulsehq.com/feeds/warning-letters.json`).
3. **Live Multi-Endpoint openFDA Enforcement Recall Feed**: Query live `food`, `drug`, and `device` enforcement recalls by ingredient, firm, or vertical with clean empty-result handling.
